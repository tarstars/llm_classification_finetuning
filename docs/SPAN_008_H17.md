# H17: external data prepared; its labels need a separate training objective

Audited two sources. The original LMSYS conversation source is gated and was
inspected as metadata only. No account-sharing conditions were accepted. The open
[AllenAI cleaned UltraFeedback dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/ultrafeedback_binarized_cleaned)
was downloaded at pinned revisionf304ce59671d155273c861746581faa354d8a9af:
60829train_prefs rows,219203601bytes, MIT license declared by the card.

The data contain synthetic GPT-4 preferences, rather than the competition's
human three-class votes.3808rows have equal chosen/rejected scores,359have
identical answers and151have an empty answer. None provides a valid human tie
label. The original annotation provenance is described in the
[UltraFeedback card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/openbmb/UltraFeedback).

Exact prompt matches affected13external rows against training,3against development
and2against confirmation. Exact answer overlap affected1764,814and1116external
rows respectively; generic repeated answers can account for many such matches,
so these counts are not a claim of leaked preference targets.

The bounded near-duplicate audit indexed every original prompt and answer and
queried every external prompt/answer. Candidate retrieval uses eight minimum
hashes of five-word shingles, followed by exact Jaccard verification at0.8.
It detected273prompt pairs and619answer pairs. This approximate retrieval has
no guaranteed recall; short texts get exact matching only, and semantic overlap
is untested. Detected overlaps and invalid/ambiguous synthetic pairs are purged,
leaving **54926provisionally usable examples**. These are inputs for a future
experiment, not newly validated human training labels.

Proposed follow-up is a separately budgeted auxiliary decisive-preference stage
with at most20%external batches, randomized answer order, then finetuning on human
votes. Equal synthetic scores must not become human tie targets. Compare against
a matched human-only training control with accepted rows/tokens and fixed groups.
No mixed-data GPU training or score gain is claimed in v8; H17 remains deferred
after completing this preparation.

CPU audit elapsed881.0seconds including its original stopped attempt and recovery;
recovered peakRSS1.510GiB. The first attempt was stopped before retaining an
unbounded cache of full answer shingles. Recovery kept the same original two-hour
clock, retained a bounded retrieval index and verified candidates on demand.
Zero GPU seconds. Per-row overlap records and safe-row indices remain private.
