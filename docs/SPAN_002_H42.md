# H42: conditional tie-preserving adaptation stopped at the calibration gate

The [predeclared protocol](SPAN_002_PROTOCOL.md) requires H40 and E01 before an
anchoring experiment and stops without GPU training when calibration practically
resolves the observed paired-model tradeoff. Both prerequisites are now complete:
[H40](SPAN_002_H40.md) and [E01](SPAN_002_E01.md).

H40's nested grouped calibration on 1,024 shared dev rows gives paired CE+KL loss
**0.976835** versus frozen classifier **0.976986**, a gap of **-0.000151**.
Tie-class loss gap is **+0.001537**. Both satisfy the predefined tolerances
(overall at most +0.001, tie class at most +0.02).

**Decision:** the conditional test is complete at its CPU gate. Do not run the
two-arm tie-anchor training comparison. No GPU time was used for H42, no anchored
model was trained, and no claim is made about whether such training could help in
another regime. The practical gate is not an equivalence test; these are
exploratory results on inspected dev data. Tie discrimination remains unresolved.

The recorded budget can remain unused or be explicitly reallocated within the
original span cap. Preserve H42's original claim and the actual gate outcome.
