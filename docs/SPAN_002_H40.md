# H40: cached calibration results

Completed under the [span-002 protocol](SPAN_002_PROTOCOL.md), configuration seed
20261119. No GPU or new model training. [Aggregate results](../results/span_002/calibration_results.json)
record parameters, source hashes, exact family controls and resource use; private
fold assignments and per-row predictions are under the span-002 heavy run directory.

Five prompt-grouped outer folds evaluate all families. Three inner grouped folds
select temperature, temperature-plus-tie or symmetric-vector calibration separately
within each outer training partition. The nested selector is primary; family losses
are additional declared diagnostics. Existing development has been inspected and
results remain exploratory. Confirmation was not evaluated.

Full-classifier nested gain over matched temperature is **0.0011325**, 95% paired
group interval **[-0.0005643, 0.0029542]**, across 5,211 groups. It is a small,
uncertain improvement, not evidence of a large score gain. No candidate is promoted
and the official Kaggle best remains **0.97025**.

On the common 1,024 rows / 990 groups, calibration reduces the paired CE+KL versus
frozen loss gap to **-0.0001510**. Their tie-class losses become **1.075729** and
**1.074192**, a gap of **0.0015366**. Both pass the predeclared H42 stopping
tolerances (overall gap at most +0.001; tie-class gap at most +0.02). This diagnostic
practically resolves the previously observed calibration tradeoff; it is not an
equivalence proof or a demonstrated improvement in tie discrimination.

The frozen classifier's own calibration changes its tie-class loss from 1.026904
to 1.074192 while improving A/B contributions; the paired model moves the other
way, from tie-class loss 1.172416 to 1.075729. Equalizing the models does not mean
every class improved. Class contributions in the JSON sum to overall gain.

**Decision:** finish H40 with a small inconclusive gain; use its gate evidence to
avoid H42 GPU anchoring once prerequisite E01 is resolved. Preserve the paired
weights and original temperature controls. Any later combined calibration/ensemble
or discrimination experiment belongs to a new evidenced proposal, not an unrecorded
variant in this test.
